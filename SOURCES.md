## What "original" means

No single autograph/original manuscript survives to download. The BORI critical text is a scholarly reconstruction assembled from manuscript comparisons. The Sanskrit of older popular/vulgate editions and the Southern recension may differ in verses, stories and numbering. The Harivaṃśa is treated as a supplement, not one of the 18 constituent parvas in this reader.

The project holds to ONE primary numbering system: BORI parva.adhyaya.verse, with original-source spelling retained.

## Source-unit segmentation rules

01.001.000 is an opening invocation marked 01001000a / 01001000c. It must not be silently treated as ordinary verse 1.

01.001.001 is marked 01001001A in the source, indicating prose. It is continued syntactically in the following śloka.

01.001.002 is formed from both 01001002a and 01001002c. Some verses have additional pādas (such as e) and must be assembled completely. Speaker-attribution lines without a suffix are not automatically a verse pāda.

## Source rights

The electronic files explicitly declare: "Electronic text (C) Bhandarkar Oriental Research Institute, Pune, India, 1999". The complete electronic text is linked, not mirrored on GitHub. Seek explicit permission or an appropriate licence before wholesale republication. The source downloader saves to an ignored local directory and is never part of GitHub Pages publication.

Our independently authored English and Hindi translations and commentary remain distinct editorial content. A quotation of ancient Sanskrit does not erase the electronic source's attribution.

## Editorial quality gate

1. Verify exact Sanskrit spelling and all pādas against the correct source ID.
2. Translate each unit faithfully in English and Hindi, preserving named speakers and syntactic continuation across verse boundaries.
3. Modern context is a clearly marked explanation, never inserted into translation.
4. Teachings remain separate, and are omitted when a narrative unit does not justify them.
5. Date each batch, record source URL, update translation last ID and the next pending ID.
6. No invented Sanskrit, source references, chapter titles, completion claims or moral teachings.
7. Review uncertain transliterations and interpretations before a passage is called fully verified.
